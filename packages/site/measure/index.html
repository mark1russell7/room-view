Measure a room

Walk around the room with a tape measure, and keep the wall on your right. Type the length of each wall and the turn at each corner. Then add the doors, the windows and the outlets of each wall. The page draws the room while you type.

        Keep your work

        The page saves each change in this browser, and it sends your numbers to no server. A file moves the room that the page shows to a different device or to a program. With All rooms selected, a file has each room and the doors between them.

        How to measure

        1. Stand in a corner of the room, with a wall on your right.
        2. Measure this wall along the floor, from corner to corner. Type its length.
        3. For each door and each window of this wall, measure from a corner of the wall to its near edge. Then measure its width.
        4. For each outlet of this wall, measure from a corner of the wall to its center. Then measure its height above the floor.
        5. Go to the next corner and turn to the next wall. In most corners you turn left. Around a corner that points into the room, you turn right.
        6. For a corner that is not square, select Angle. Type the angle inside the room, between the two walls.
        7. Continue until you are at the first corner again. The page tells if the walk ends where it started.
        8. For the next room, select Add a room. The page keeps each room, and the list above the drawing shows them.
        9. Measure a door between two rooms in each of the two rooms. At one of the two doors, select the other door in the field Goes to.
        10. Select All rooms to see the rooms together. The page tells if the rooms fit at each door.

        The measurements are a survey/1 record, and the package makes the room from it. The rooms together are a level/1 record.
